## Pattern
**Isometric illustrations as one plane matrix.** An isometric badge scene uses only two ground axes, here U = (0.857, -0.514) and V = (0.857, 0.514) in screen px. Each slab is a corner point plus two edge lengths along U and V and a thickness straight down, so five cards fit in a ten-line generator. Logos and lettering go on a face by drawing them flat and wrapping them in `matrix(Ux,Uy,Vx,Vy,x,y)` for top faces, or `matrix(Ux,Uy,0,1,x,y)` for vertical faces. A round puck's seal is a plain circle under the same top-face matrix. Light that travels along a wire is a dash on a copy of the wire path. Its dashoffset moves at a measured px/s, and its delay is solved from one tracked point, so the pulse lands where the clip has it.
